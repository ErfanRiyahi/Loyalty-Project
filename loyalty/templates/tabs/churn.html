Churn Prediction — Risk Assessment

Use the latest Monthly Learning model to estimate next-month activity decline. The other methods provide score-based risk assessments.

The trained method uses the latest Monthly Learning output. Upload and path fields below apply only to the two score-based methods.
Use your cleaned, scored customer result with MSISDN and at least two informative score columns. Upload takes priority over the path. A D:/ path works only when the Python server can access that drive.

Higher scores mean stronger loyalty. The experimental method gives each of six score groups one vote; ARPU, CLV and Growth share one vote. At least two informative groups are required. High ≥ 67; Medium ≥ 34; Low < 34. These thresholds are heuristic.

Missing evidence produces a possible-risk range. The method marks “Needs review” when that range crosses a band boundary or removing a group changes the band. These are sensitivity checks, not statistical confidence intervals. The baseline remains available for comparison.
